How high can the fine go for your company?

The European rules set a fixed amount or a share of worldwide annual revenue, whichever is higher. Move the slider and watch where the percentage takes over.

The percentage appliesThe fixed amount applies

The GDPR's higher tier covers core principles, people's rights and international transfers; the lower tier covers duties such as security and breach notice. Under the AI Act, small and medium companies get the lower of the two amounts, not the higher. LGPD fines are capped per violation, so several violations can add up.

HIPAA fines depend on what you knew

HIPAA sorts violations into four tiers by culpability. Pick a tier and a number of violations of the same requirement in one year.

The annual cap applies per identical provision, so a single breach that violates several requirements can carry several caps. HHS has used a 2019 enforcement policy with lower annual caps for the first three tiers; both caps are shown. Criminal penalties are separate: up to $250,000 and 10 years in prison when information is obtained to sell or for harm.

Start the breach clock

Set the moment your team discovers a breach. Each rule that applies to you starts its own deadline from there.

Business days skip weekends but not public holidays. The SEC clock starts when a public company decides the incident is material, which here is assumed to be the moment of discovery. Visa expects notice to your bank and Visa immediately; the date shown is when your findings are due.
